# GTM-agents

A general-purpose, multi-agent go-to-market (GTM) system that anyone can adapt to their own product.

> **Status:** Early stage. This README is a placeholder and will change as the project takes shape.

## What this is

GTM-agents is meant to be a reusable framework of cooperating AI agents that help teams take a product to market. Instead of a one-off playbook tied to a single company, the system is designed to be product-agnostic. You describe your product, audience, and goals, and the agents handle the repetitive parts of GTM work.

## Intended scope

These examples are illustrative, not final. The system may eventually cover areas such as:

- **Market and competitor research:** mapping the landscape and how alternatives are positioned
- **ICP and positioning:** defining target customers, personas, and messaging
- **Content and campaigns:** drafting launch assets, emails, and social content
- **Outbound and pipeline:** prospect research and personalized outreach
- **Measurement:** tracking what's working and feeding results back into the loop
