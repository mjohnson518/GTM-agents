# Facts

These are the only facts a reply or an outreach email may state.

## quarantine-automatic
- text: Acme detects tests that pass and fail on the same commit and quarantines them automatically.

## rerun-report
- text: Acme can produce a report of reruns caused by flaky tests from the last month of CI runs.
- value: last month of CI runs

## setup-time
- text: Most teams finish setup in under an hour.
- value: under an hour

## read-only-access
- text: Acme reads test reports only and never needs access to source code.

## owner-assignment
- text: Every quarantined test gets an owner and a history of its failures.

## security-report
- text: Acme holds a current SOC 2 Type II report, available under NDA.
- url: https://acme.example/security
- expires: 2027-06-30
