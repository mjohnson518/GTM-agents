# Voice

## plain
- kind: do
- text: Write like one engineer talking to another: short sentences, specific numbers, no hype.

## one-question
- kind: do
- text: End with one easy question the reader can answer in a line.

## no-buzzwords
- kind: dont
- text: Never use words like revolutionary, seamless, best-in-class or game-changing.

## no-fake-familiarity
- kind: dont
- text: Do not pretend to know the reader or their team; refer only to what is public or in the contact's record.

## signal-phrase
- kind: phrase
- text: A red build should mean a real problem.

## avoid-guilt
- kind: avoid
- text: Avoid guilt-trip follow-ups such as "just checking in" or "did you see my last email".

## signoff
- kind: signoff
- text: Sign with the first name only, no title or tagline.
